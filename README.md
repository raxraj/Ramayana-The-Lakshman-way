# लक्ष्मणायनम् - The Lakshman Way
*The Ramayana retold through the eyes of Lakshmana*

This markdown-based repository contains a retelling of the Ramayana from Lakshmana's perspective, exploring the untold story of the devoted brother who chose to live in service to Rama's destiny.

## About This Work

**लक्ष्मणायनम्** (Lakshmanayan) presents the epic Ramayana through the voice of Lakshmana, examining themes of:
- **Purposeful invisibility** - How true service makes the served appear effortless
- **Deliberate devotion** - The conscious choice to support another's greatness
- **Unsung heroism** - The courage to remain unnamed while enabling legends
- **Sacred brotherhood** - Bonds that transcend individual glory

## Reading Guide

This work draws inspiration from various Ramayana traditions while presenting bold new interpretations of familiar events. Sanskrit chapter titles are provided with English translations to honor the epic's cultural roots while remaining accessible to modern readers.

## Vision

*"For every shaft Rama loosed, a thousand of my silent footfalls cleared his path; for every chant of 'Siyavar Ram Chandra ki Jai,' an echo of my name dissolved in the forest wind. Today I claim that echo and shape it into story."*
